# Outreach routine

About 15 minutes a weekday. Track everything in the hub's **Outreach** tab.

## Who

HR, people, office and operations managers at Cardiff and South Wales organisations with roughly 20–500 staff: housing associations, law and property firms, manufacturers, agencies, schools and colleges, NHS and public bodies.
**Not** finance or advisory firms (too close to John's work).

## Daily

1. **LinkedIn (from Dad's account):** 5 connection requests. Prefer 2nd-degree contacts with mutual connections. Custom notes are limited on a free account (a few a month): save them for the best fits.
2. **Accepted?** Send the follow-up message (below) and set the status to *Followed up*.
3. **Due today:** the Overview tab lists anyone whose next action is due.

## Weekly

- 2–3 Facebook group posts, from a personal account where the group allows it, in the morning (8–9am).
- 5–10 emails to office or HR managers.
- Message a few people you know personally: it's still the fastest route to an enquiry.

## Messages

**LinkedIn note (under 200 characters):**
> Hi [Name], I'm Mark from Grimes Engravers, a Cardiff family business since 1947. We engrave crystal awards for staff awards and long service. Thought it'd be good to connect. Mark

**Follow-up after accepting:**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. In case it's ever useful, our awards are at grimesengravers.com/corporate-glass-awards. Free proof, and nothing to pay until you've approved it. Mark

## Don't

- Send more than ~10 LinkedIn requests a day (the account can get restricted).
- Chase pending requests; leave them.
- Post the same text in many groups on the same day.
