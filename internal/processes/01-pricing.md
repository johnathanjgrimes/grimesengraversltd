# Pricing

How we price glass awards. The website shows these as a guide; the quote confirms the final price.

## What the customer pays

| Part | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Award | Website price | Already includes our margin (about double the trade price on most awards) |
| Engraving | **£10.50 per award, per position** | Text and/or logo, frosted (sandblasted) finish, no colour. Front and back = 2 positions |
| Setup | **£40 flat, once per order** | Even when every award has a different name (Dad does one proof) |
| Delivery | Quoted with the order | See "Delivery" below |
| Bulk discount | **20+ awards** | Shown as its own line in the quote, e.g. "Bulk discount: −£120" |

**We are not VAT registered:** never show or add VAT. We pay VAT to the supplier and can't reclaim it, so our real costs include it.

## Our real costs (Crystal Galleries)

| Part | Trade (ex VAT) | Our real cost (inc VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Award | Trade price on Crystal Galleries (logged in) | Trade × 1.2 |
| Engraving | £2.50 per award | £3.00 |
| Origination / setup | £17.50 per design | £21.00 |
| Carriage | Minimum £12.65, confirmed per order | Roughly £6.25 + £1.51 per kg (ex VAT), so about £15–£55 inc VAT for most orders |

Buying 24+ of one award takes about 4% off its trade price.

## Discounts

- Default for 20+ awards: **10% off the total**, as its own line.
- Up to **15%** is fine on big or important orders (still about 30%+ margin).
- Never go below the cheapest competitor *with VAT added* unless there's a good reason (repeat customer, foot in the door).
- Show the discount clearly: a visible discount feels more generous than a lower total.

## Quick margin check

Real cost ≈ (trade × qty × 1.2) + (qty × £3) + £21 + carriage (inc VAT).
Customer price ≈ (website price × qty) + (qty × £10.50) + £40 − discount.
Aim for **30%+** of the customer price as profit.

## Competitors (Oct 2026)

Online promo sellers are roughly 10–25% cheaper on the same awards once their VAT is added (e.g. Wills & Merwagg include setup and delivery). Our edge: no VAT to add, local family firm, a free proof, a real person, hand-picked options.
