Our supplier for glass and crystal awards.

## What they charge us

- **Award:** trade price (about half our website price on most awards); 24+ of one award saves about 4%.
- **Engraving ("branding"):** £2.50 per award + VAT.
- **Origination ("Engraved 1 design"):** £17.50 per order/design + VAT.
- **Carriage:** the online basket always shows a **£12.65 minimum, "to be confirmed"**. The real figure is worked out by them afterwards and goes up with weight (e.g. £13.80 for about 5 kg, £25.30 for about 12.6 kg).
- Production: 7–10 working days, then next-day delivery.

## Questions to ask them

- Bulk or project pricing beyond 4% at 24+?
- A carriage rate table (by weight or box), or a fixed rate for orders like ours?
- A trade price feed (CSV or API) for stockists?
- Is it OK that we've saved their templates for our proofs?
- Can they refer Cardiff enquiries to us as a local stockist?

## Don't

- Automate their basket or checkout (it's Mark's trade account, and it can't tell us real carriage anyway).
- Call parts of their website the shop doesn't normally use.
