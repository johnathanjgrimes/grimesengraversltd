# Handling an order or enquiry

From the moment an order arrives to the awards going out.

## 1. It arrives

- **Website order:** an email from the order form. It lists the awards, quantities, wording and the customer's details, plus a **reopen_this_order** link.
- **Plain email enquiry** (no order form): go to the hub's **Options links** tab and build a suggested-options link for them (step 3).

Reply **the same working day** if you can. Speed wins orders.

## 2. Check stock

- Open the **reopen_this_order** link in a **private window**: each award or option shows live stock.
- Or check on Crystal Galleries (logged in as Mark).
- Short on stock? Suggest the closest in-stock alternative (similar shape and size).

## 3. Not sure what they want? Send suggested options

In the hub → **Options links**:
1. Short name (e.g. `wwh-staff-awards`), organisation, needed-by date.
2. Two or three options at different budgets, one per line: `Name | SY5067 x 14, SY5066 x 14 | description`.
3. **Check stock and save**. It won't save if anything is short.
4. **Publish now**, wait a few minutes, test the link in a private window.
5. **Personal link** (adds their name and email so the form is filled in) → send it in a short, friendly email.

## 4. Quote

Use the order email (it shows estimated totals per option). Then:
- Confirm award prices and stock.
- Add the **bulk discount line** for 20+ awards (see Pricing).
- Add **delivery**.
- Say: free proof first; once approved, a **deposit or full payment** before we engrave; purchase orders welcome.
- Give the **deadline**: 10–14 working days after the proof is approved.

## 5. Proof

Hub → **Proofs** → glass award (product code + wording) or bench plaque (size, metal, font, emblem, wording).
For a set, put a blank line between each award's wording so they all match.
Check it, then email the PDF.

## 6. Approved and paid

- Deposit or full payment received → order from Crystal Galleries (awards + engraving + one origination charge).
- Note the expected delivery date.

## 7. Delivered

- A friendly check that everything arrived well.
- Ask for a **Google review** and permission to share a photo.
- Diary a note for **next year's event** (most staff awards are annual).
